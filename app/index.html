      How it works

      1. Crop

        The photo's short side is resized to 512 pixels, and the middle 448 × 448 square is kept. Training scored its test photos the same way.

      2. Two networks

        ResNet-50 and ConvNeXt-Tiny, both CNNs pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on 4,829 bird photos, each give a probability for all 200 species.

      3. Average

        The two probability lists are averaged with equal weight. The most likely species after averaging is the answer.

      4. In your browser

        Both networks run in this browser with onnxruntime-web, on the graphics card when it can.

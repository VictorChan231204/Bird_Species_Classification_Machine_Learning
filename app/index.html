      How it works

      1. Photo Short side resized to 512 px, middle 448 × 448 kept
      2. ConvNeXt-Tiny A CNN with 28 million weights: layers of filters find edges, then textures, then bird parts
      3. 200 probabilities Softmax turns its 200 scores into probabilities that sum to 100%
      4. Answer The species with the highest probability

      Where its knowledge comes from. ConvNeXt-Tiny first learned general shapes and textures from 1.28 million ImageNet photos. It was then fine-tuned on the 4,829 bird training photos for 30 epochs: every layer learned, the copied layers at a learning rate 10 times smaller than the new last layer, so they kept most of what they knew.

      How it avoids memorising. With about 24 photos per species, a network can memorise the training photos instead of learning the birds. Four tools work against that, together: strong augmentation (random crops, turns and colour changes), label smoothing, weight decay and dropout.

      Limitations

      • It knows only 200 species. Any other bird, or a photo with no bird, still gets one of the 200 names.
      • It learned from about 24 photos per species, and it sees only the middle square of the photo.

      Details

      Model
      Ensemble of two CNNs. Each gives a probability for all 200 species; the two lists are averaged with equal weight, and the highest is the answer.
      Networks
      ResNet-50 (23.9 million weights) and ConvNeXt-Tiny (28.0 million weights), both starting from torchvision's ImageNet-1k weights.
      Input
      One colour photo. The short side is resized to 512 pixels and the middle 448 × 448 square is kept.
      Output
      200 probabilities, one per species.
      Training
      Every layer fine-tuned for 30 epochs: SGD with momentum 0.9, warm-up then cosine learning rate, label smoothing 0.1, weight decay, dropout 0.3, strong augmentation.
      Runs in
      This browser, with onnxruntime-web: on the graphics card when it can, else on the processor. Two ONNX files, 104.7 MB, downloaded once per visit.

      Training data

      Caltech-UCSD Birds 200 (CUB-200, 2010 version): 6,033 photos of 200 species. 4,829 are training photos, about 24 per species; 1,204 are test photos.

      During experiments 1 to 7, 800 training photos were held back as validation photos to choose the settings. The final model then trained on all 4,829. The test photos never chose anything.

      Intended use

      A coursework demonstration: name the species of one bird in a photo, out of the 200 species in CUB-200.

      It is not a field guide. Check a real sighting against a trusted guide.

      Limitations

      • It knows only 200 species. Any other bird, or a photo with no bird, still gets one of the 200 names.
      • It learned from about 24 photos per species, so rare poses and lighting can fool it.
      • It looks only at the middle square of the photo. A bird near the edge may be cut off.
      • The test photos come from the same collection as the training photos. Photos from other sources may score lower (an estimate, not measured).
